Model assumptions and interpretation

Baseline allows attrition and diversion to reduce staffing. Hire replaces losses and closes staffing gaps through a first-order adjustment. Protect + hire additionally reduces diversions at an annual cost. A persistent diversion shock starts at the configured shock year. Certification follows completion of design work. Orders and production ramp begin after certification. Aircraft quantities are continuous SD approximations.

All money is constant USD million. NPV includes initial capex and discounted operating cashflows, with no terminal value. Net cash is cumulative project cashflow, not corporate bank cash. Exit, financing, taxes, competitor reactions and DAPP pathways are not evaluated here. The design and certification plots use a 0–100% scale; other plots scale to the three policies at the selected staffing level.
